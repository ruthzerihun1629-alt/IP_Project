Frequently Asked Questions

How can I rent a vehicle?
Browse available vehicles, select your preferred vehicle, and follow the booking process.

Can I choose my rental dates?
Yes. You can provide your preferred pickup and return dates when searching for vehicles.

How can I contact customer support?
Visit our Contact Us page to find our contact information and send us a message.
What types of vehicles are available?
We offer different types of vehicles, including sedans, SUVs, and family vehicles to meet different transportation needs.

Can I cancel my booking?
Yes. You can request a booking cancellation by contacting our customer support team.

Do I need a driving license to rent a vehicle?
Yes. Customers must have a valid driving license to rent and drive a vehicle.
